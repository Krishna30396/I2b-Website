Questions founders ask

FAQ

What stage do you invest at?

Pre-seed: pre-product or pre-revenue. We prefer to come in before the risk is priced in.

Do I need a technical co-founder?

No. Our engineers build alongside you; we'll help you hire your own team as the company grows.

Which sectors do you focus on?

Structural problems in under-digitised industries: supply chain and logistics, growth and marketing, finance, healthcare and communication.

Do you work outside Hyderabad?

Our studio is in Hyderabad and we work on-site with founders during the build. Talk to us if you're elsewhere.

Do you also build platforms for businesses?

Yes, for B2B platforms in our areas of depth. Choose "Business" on the contact form.
