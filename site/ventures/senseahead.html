The problem

Supply chains run on yesterday's spreadsheets.

Disruptions, supplier failures and regulatory changes reach most teams through email and spreadsheets, after the damage is done. Risk, forecasting and compliance usually live in separate tools that don't talk to each other.

SenseAhead brings them into one system that flags risk early, models what happens next, and checks trade compliance in the same place.
