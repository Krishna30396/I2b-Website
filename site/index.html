We lead the build early, then hand you the wheel.

Five stages from idea to company. Our hands-on role shrinks as your own team grows.

    Problem

    Find the structural problem worth a company.

    Sector research, operator context
    You leave with

  2. Stage 2

    I2B brings

    You leave with

  3. Stage 3

Are we a fit?

We back a few founders a year. Here's who.

We're a fit if you…

  • Are pre-product or pre-revenueWe come in before the risk is priced in.
  • Are solving a structural problemIndustries where technology hasn't arrived yet.
  • Think platform-firstBuilt for scale from day one, not bolted on later.
  • Want category leadershipYou're playing the long game, not the next round.

Probably not if you…

  • –
    Only need capitalWe're hands-on; we build alongside you.
  • –
    Want an agency to build to specWe co-own the outcome, not a statement of work.
  • –
    Are optimising for a quick exitWe measure in years and unit economics.

Meaningful companies are built through disciplined execution, sound unit economics and an obsessive focus on users.That's the bar we hold ourselves to, and the one we look for in founders.
